# 0.13.0 Waking Sky Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** built natively in one session (the owner's standing choice), then one lean review. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** new land each time the screensaver opens, and a sky that rests, lights on approach, opens the board only on a touch, and goes dark after an adjustable wait.

**Architecture:** pure rules in `modules/screensaver/sky.ts` (landscape) and `core/screensaver.ts` (stages, Screen calls); the page-wide `ScreensaverController` works out the stage each second and tells the element; `pv-screensaver` draws the stage with one shade layer and stops the scene in the dark; the backend stores one new field.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript + Lit, vitest; Python, pytest in Docker.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-waking-sky-design.md`

## Global constraints

- No em dashes in tracked text (`python scripts/check_copy.py`).
- Versions match: `manifest.json`, `frontend/package.json`, `const.py` `VERSION` (0.13.0).
- Data stays rollback-safe: add fields only, keep unknown fields.
- The shade animates opacity only; nothing redraws a canvas for it.
- New copy never says OK for approval; sentence case in Settings.

## Review focus

- A motion sensor stuck on: the sky stays lit and never goes dark; the board still opens on a touch.
- The Screen entity unavailable at dark or on waking: nothing is lost; the put-back waits for a known state (0.11.0's hold).
- A touch on a dark panel (backlight off): opens the board and turns the Screen back on.
- Two pages of one login: the record keeps one hold; a touch on either wakes both.
- A reload while dark: comes back up dark (the record says showing), Screen kept off.
